Asian Games 2026 — Aichi-Nagoya
1. General Information
The 20th Asian Games, officially known as the 20th Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya 2026, are being held in Japan.
The Games take place from September 19 to October 4, 2026.
The host region is Aichi Prefecture, with Nagoya serving as the principal host city.
The Games are being organized across multiple locations in Japan rather than being confined to a single city. The official organizers list 54 competition venues.
Additional competition venues are located in:
Aichi Prefecture
Gifu Prefecture
Shizuoka Prefecture
Tokyo
Osaka
The official Games website lists 43 sports for the 2026 Asian Games.
