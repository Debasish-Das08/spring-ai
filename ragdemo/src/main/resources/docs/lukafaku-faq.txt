# LUKA FAKU — Hide and Seek FAQ

### FAQ 1: What is LUKA FAKU?

LUKA FAKU is a hide-and-seek game where one player searches for the other players while they hide within a designated playing area.

### FAQ 2: How many players are needed?

LUKA FAKU can be played with a minimum of 3 players, but it becomes more enjoyable with 5 or more players.

### FAQ 3: Who is the seeker?

The seeker is the player whose job is to find all the hidden players before the round ends.

### FAQ 4: How is the seeker selected?

The seeker can be selected randomly, through a counting game, or by mutual agreement among the players.

### FAQ 5: Where can LUKA FAKU be played?

It can be played in a safe indoor or outdoor area such as a playground, garden, school premises, or large house.

### FAQ 6: How long does a round last?

A typical round can last between 5 and 15 minutes. Players can decide the time limit before starting.

### FAQ 7: What happens when the seeker finds someone?

When the seeker finds a hidden player, the seeker must identify or tag that player according to the agreed rules.

### FAQ 8: Can players change hiding places?

Players may change their hiding places only if the rules of that particular round allow movement after hiding.

### FAQ 9: How does the seeker win?

The seeker wins by finding all the hidden players before the time limit expires.

### FAQ 10: How do the hiders win?

The hiders win if at least one hidden player remains undiscovered when the round ends.

### FAQ 11: Are there safe zones?

Yes. Players can create a designated safe zone where a hider cannot be tagged. The location and rules of the safe zone should be agreed upon before the game.

### FAQ 12: Can players hide outside the playing area?

No. Players must remain inside the predefined playing area for safety and fairness.

### FAQ 13: What happens in the next round?

Usually, the first player found becomes the seeker in the next round, although players can choose another method.

### FAQ 14: Is LUKA FAKU suitable for children?

Yes, provided the playing area is safe, boundaries are clearly defined, and an adult supervises younger children when necessary.

### FAQ 15: What is the most important rule?

Players should stay within the agreed boundaries, avoid dangerous hiding places, and follow the rules decided before the round begins.
